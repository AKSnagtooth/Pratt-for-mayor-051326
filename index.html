Spencer's Plan

Real plans. Not slogans.

Seven priorities. Real solutions. The work starts Day One.

01

Crisis Leadership & Emergency Readiness

Show up when LA needs leadership most. Pre-position resources before fire season. Cut bureaucracy that's kept 99.5% of Palisades families from rebuilding.

02

Fiscal Integrity & Government Reform

Audit every city department. Stop new spending until existing programs deliver results. Replace political pork with performance metrics.

03

Public Safety & the Rule of Law

Restore LAPD staffing and back the officers who keep LA safe. Prosecute property crime and retail theft. End the catch-and-release approach.

04

Treatment-First Approach to Homelessness

Mandatory drug and mental health treatment for those who refuse housing. End the multi-billion-dollar industry that's grown while the streets got worse.

05

Infrastructure & Quality of Life

Fix the roads. Pick up the trash. Repair the sidewalks. Cut permit delays. The basics that LA forgot how to do.

06

Economic Opportunity & Small Business

Cut business permit times in half. Reform the gross receipts tax. Bring entertainment production back from Atlanta and Toronto. Reward people who build.

07

Utility Accountability & Ratepayer Protection

Audit LADWP. Cap rate increases. Investigate utility decisions that worsened the Palisades fire. Modernize the grid before the next disaster.
