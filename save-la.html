My Story

The day LA failed all of us.

On January 7, 2025, I stood on my street and watched my neighborhood burn.

Karen Bass was in Ghana. We were screaming for help. The hydrants had no water. Fire trucks were stranded. The mayor of LA was 8,000 miles away on a trip she'd been warned not to take.

I lost my home. So did 6,800 of my neighbors. Sixteen months later, only 0.5% of us have rebuilt — Palisades lags every comparable California wildfire recovery, including Paradise after Camp Fire and Santa Rosa after Tubbs. People are still in hotel rooms. Insurance companies are still saying no. The city that should have shown up for us never did.

I'm not a politician. I never planned to run for anything. I'm a husband and father who watched the system fail in real time. And I realized something: if I don't run, who will?

This isn't about me. It's about the next family whose house burns. The next neighborhood Bass abandons. The next four years of a city that's forgotten how to function.

LA is worth saving. But it won't save itself.

I'd vote for you.

Spencer's Plan

Real solutions. Day one.

Seven priorities. Not slogans. Things that actually work in cities that haven't given up.

01

Crisis Leadership & Emergency Readiness

Never again. Real wildfire preparedness with real accountability. Working hydrants. Pre-positioned resources. A mayor who shows up.

02

Fiscal Integrity & Government Reform

End the waste. Audit every department. The $14.85B budget needs to serve Angelenos, not bureaucrats and consultants.

03

Public Safety & the Rule of Law

Streets safe for families. Real consequences for criminals. A city where you don't have to look over your shoulder.

04

Treatment-First Approach to Homelessness

Zero encampments. Real treatment for addiction and mental health. The current system enriches contractors. We end that.

05

Infrastructure & Quality of Life

Fix the streets. Restore the parks. Make LA camera-ready again, the way it used to be.

06

Economic Opportunity & Small Business

Cut the red tape choking small business. Bring back the production jobs Hollywood is losing every month. LA's economy needs a fighter.

07

Utility Accountability & Ratepayer Protection

End the LADWP corruption. Lower rates. Hold the people who failed us during the fire accountable.
